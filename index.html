Load a design to review jumps.

Selected travel runs receive thread-cut commands; the travel itself is not removed. Optional cleanup removes consecutive zero-length stitch commands only. Other geometry is preserved. Export is reopened and checked for needle position, stitch count, color-change count and dimensions; files failing these checks are blocked. Some machine formats do not encode explicit trims or may approximate them. Re-exporting can change metadata and thread colors. Always inspect the output in your embroidery software and test on scrap fabric before sewing a final item.
