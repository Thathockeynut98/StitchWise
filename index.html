✂   Repair Design Studio

Preview conservative fixes, manage trims, and export a revised design

NON-DESTRUCTIVE
Preview changes before exporting. Stitch travel is not removed by trimming.
Load a design to review jumps.

Selected travel runs receive thread-cut commands; the travel itself is not removed. Optional cleanup removes consecutive zero-length stitch commands only. Other geometry is preserved. Some machine formats do not encode explicit trims or may approximate them. Re-exporting can change metadata and thread colors. Always inspect the output in your embroidery software and test on scrap fabric before sewing a final item.

✧   Repair Advisor

Contextual, rule-based repair recommendations

LOCAL ADVISOR
Select a finding to see a practical explanation and recommended fix.
The decoder loads external runtime packages for additional formats, but design bytes are processed locally. This version uses deterministic stitch rules, not a hosted generative AI model. Suggestions are screening guidance and must be validated by an experienced digitizer and test sew-out. Experimental conservative edits can be exported from Repair Design Studio; advanced density and travel optimization are not implemented.
